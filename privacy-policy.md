# Vibe Check Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 22, 2026

Vibe Check: Local Vibes ("Vibe Check") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Vibe Check mobile application.

## Location Information

Vibe Check may collect precise location information when you grant the app permission to access your location. Location information is used to provide app functionality, including showing nearby places and local vibes.

Your precise location is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you across apps or websites.

You can control or disable location access at any time through your device's Settings.

## Personal Information

Vibe Check does not require you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number to use the app.

## Tracking and Advertising

Vibe Check does not use your location information to track you across apps or websites. Vibe Check does not sell your personal information.

## Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Children's Privacy

Vibe Check does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Vibe Check changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
